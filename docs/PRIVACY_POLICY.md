# Privacy Policy — Kids Explorer AI

_Last updated: [DATE]. Replace bracketed text before publishing._

Kids Explorer AI ("the app", "the website") is made by **[Your name / company]**, [address], contact **[email]**. It is designed for children and their families. We take children's privacy seriously and follow COPPA, GDPR (including Article 8) and the Google Play Families Policy.

## 1. No accounts, no personal information
The app has **no sign-up and no login**. We do **not** ask for or collect a child's email, phone number, address, photos, location or contacts.

The child's **first name / nickname, age group, chosen hero and theme**, stars, stickers and learning progress are stored **only on the device** (browser storage or the phone's app storage). They are never sent to us. A parent can erase them anytime in **Grown-ups → Erase all data on this device**, or by uninstalling the app / clearing site data.

## 2. Anonymous usage statistics
If enabled by us, the app sends anonymous events such as "a story was finished", "a game was won", together with the age group, platform and selected theme. These events contain **no name, no device identifier, no advertising ID and no precise location**. We use them only to improve content.

## 3. Microphone and speech
The Talking Buddy and pronunciation games use the microphone **only while the child taps the 🎤 button**. Speech is converted to text by the device's built-in speech service (e.g. Google speech services on Android, or the browser's speech service) and is **not recorded or stored by us**. [If cloud AI is enabled: the typed/recognized sentence — without the child's name — is sent to our server and an AI provider ([provider]) to generate a reply and is not stored.]

## 4. Advertising (Android app)
The Android app may show ads from **Google AdMob**. All ad requests are marked as **child-directed**, are **non-personalized**, limited to **G-rated** content, and the app does **not** access the Android advertising ID. Ads are never shown inside learning activities; optional rewarded ads are only available behind a parental gate. Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## 5. Videos
Cartoon videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to video playback: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. The app does not let children leave to YouTube or other sites.
